Oakville rules we draw to.

Checked against Zoning By-law 2014-014 and the City's own pages, as of October 2026. By-laws change often: confirm with the City before you submit.

How many ADUs

On a detached lot, up to three attached ADUs, or two attached and one detached. On semi-detached, linked and townhouse lots, two attached, or one attached and one detached (By-law 2024-053).

Zoning certificate first

Oakville wants a zoning certificate of occupancy before the building permit, and the ADU is registered with the Town.

Entrances

An ADU entrance can't face a yard narrower than 1.0 m (s.4.1.3).

Below-grade stairs

In the rear yard they may encroach up to 1.5 m, staying 0.6 m from the lot line. In an interior side yard there's no encroachment, so the stair has to meet the house's required side yard (Table 4.3.1).

Parking

Tandem and stacked spaces are permitted for any dwelling (s.5.1.10).

Detached ADU

Up to 4.0 m high, 1.2 m from the interior side and rear lot lines (s.6.5.3).

Decks

A permit is needed for a deck over 10 m² and more than 61 cm above grade, or any deck attached to the house.

Trees

Trees 15 cm or more in diameter are protected, and permits go in with a tree protection declaration.

Submitting in Oakville. Oakville takes applications through ePlan. For a basement unit it asks for a site plan, elevations, floor plans with fire separations, HVAC design and water service calculations.

Questions from Oakville firms

Do you include the HVAC drawings Oakville asks for?

We draft HVAC and plumbing layouts as an add-on for your engineer or HVAC designer to seal. HVAC design and engineering are extra; ask when you book.

Will my client or the City see your name?

No. The set goes out in your title block, under your name. We never contact your client or the municipality.

Can you guarantee zoning approval?

No. We check the set against the zoning by-law for the address and flag anything tight on the job notes sheet, but approval rests with the municipality. By-laws change often, so confirm the current rules with the City before you submit.
