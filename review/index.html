What the report covers

  • Zoning for the address: setbacks, height, coverage, GFA, parking, second-unit rules, and the by-law section for each
  • 2024 OBC Part 9: stairs, guards, handrails, egress, ceiling heights, fire separations, smoke and CO alarms, spatial separation
  • Notes, dimensions and tags that don't agree between sheets
  • Details that don't match the plans and sections
  • What the municipality expects in the submission

What it isn't

A quality check to help your submission, not a permit approval, an engineering review or a design service. You stay the designer of record and decide what to change. Requirements vary by municipality, so always confirm with the city.

Sets are kept private, used only for your review, and never shown to anyone else.

    Questions

    Who does the review?

    An Ontario residential designer with years on both sides of the permit counter, using the same zoning and Code checklists we run on our own permit sets.

    What do I send?

    The full permit set as one PDF. A survey, the zoning certificate or any City comments help, so add a share link if you have them.

    How does the re-check work?

    Fix the items and send the revised set within 30 days. Pick the original review from the "Is this a re-check?" list and we check that each item was dealt with. One re-check per review, no charge.

    Can I cancel a membership?

    Yes, any time from "Billing and cancel" after you sign in. It runs to the end of the month you paid for.

    Do you keep my drawings?

    Only to do the review and the re-check. They are never shared, published or used in our marketing.
