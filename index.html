Questions

Straight answers.

Do you stamp or sign the drawings?

No. For white-label work, you are the designer of record and your BCIN or seal goes on the set. For contractor sets we arrange the designer of record where the project needs one, and the price reflects it.

Which municipalities do you cover?

The GTA: Toronto, Mississauga, Caledon, Oakville, Burlington, Milton, Halton Hills, Vaughan, Markham, Richmond Hill, Pickering, Ajax, Whitby and Oshawa. Projects in the City of Brampton are not accepted.

What do you need from me to start?

The design (sketch, markups or SD drawings with dimensions), the survey, any existing drawings for renovations, your deadline, and for firms, your Revit template and a recent permit set.

What if the examiner comes back with comments?

One round of revisions is included on every job. Comments caused by our drafting are fixed at no charge; design changes after delivery are billed at $75/hour.

How is payment handled?

First job: 50% to start, balance on delivery. After that, invoiced on delivery with 15-day terms. Booking is free; nothing is charged until you accept the quote.
