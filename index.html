Questions from firms

Will my client ever know?

No. The set goes out in your title block and line styles, under your name. We never contact your client or the municipality.

Who is responsible for the permit?

You are, as the designer or engineer of record. You review, stamp or seal, and submit. If the examiner comments, send them over and we revise. Code revisions from City comments are included at no extra cost.

What do you need from me to start?

The design (sketch, markups or schematic design drawings with dimensions), the survey, any existing drawings for renovations, your deadline. If you want the set in your own title block ($300 one-time template setup), send your template with your first job (ideally with 1–2 recent permit sets, in Revit or DWG, and your text, dimension and line styles): we set it up and send a sample sheet to approve before drafting. Otherwise we use our clean, unbranded format.

No design yet, just a description?

We can design it. Tick "No design yet" when you book and describe what your client wants. We prepare a layout for you to approve before drafting, quoted per job or at $85/hour. You approve the layout and stay the designer of record.

Do you check the Building Code?

We draft to the 2024 Ontario Building Code and flag anything we notice on the job notes sheet, but the final review for code and by-law compliance is yours as the designer of record.

What files do I get?

The PDF set and DWG files, plus a one-page job notes sheet listing our assumptions, open questions and any code or zoning concerns. Native Revit models aren't included.

How do I send a job?

Click Book a job, sign in with your email and fill in the six short steps. Upload your files or share a Drive or Dropbox link. We pre-screen your files and send your price or fixed quote within one business day.

Do you draw underpinning or basement lowering?

Yes, underpinning or benching. Send the existing basement layout and the survey, and tell us the foundation type (block, poured concrete or rubble stone). We draw the full set: staged foundation plan, sections and details, plus the basement layout if you're finishing it. Your engineer reviews and stamps it, and one round of their markups is included once the job is paid in full, if uploaded within 6 weeks of the final files. $650, or $400 added to a basement finish or second unit. Contractors without an engineer can ask us to refer one.

Do you draw sheds, cabanas, gazebos or pergolas?

Yes. Over 15 m², or with plumbing, they need a building permit: we draw the full permit set for $750. At 15 m² or less with no plumbing, most Ontario municipalities don't require a permit, so we draw build drawings instead (plan, section, framing and footing details) for $350, with a note on the first sheet that they weren't reviewed by a building official. Zoning still applies at any size, so check setbacks, height and lot coverage, and confirm with the municipality whether a permit is needed.

Can you guarantee zoning approval?

No. We check every set against the zoning by-law for the address and flag anything that looks tight, but approval rests with the municipality. Examiners can interpret the by-law differently, and by-laws are amended often, so approval can't be guaranteed. If a variance is needed, we can prepare the drawings for it at $85/hour.

Do I see the drawings before I pay the balance?

Yes. Before finals you get a watermarked, view-only draft to check that we captured everything. Tell us what to fix; once you approve and the balance is paid, we release the clean PDF and DWG files.

Can I share inspiration?

Yes. Add a Pinterest board or a link to photos when you book, so we can match the look you and your client expect.

Which municipalities do you cover?

Mississauga, Toronto, Oakville, Burlington, Brampton, Halton Hills, Innisfil, Barrie, Guelph, Kitchener, Hamilton and many more. If it's in Ontario and under Part 9 of the Building Code, send it through.
