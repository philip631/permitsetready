What it is

A first look at what a site can carry and whether it pays, so you can decide whether to buy, design or walk away before spending on full drawings.

What it isn't

It's not a permit set, a zoning approval or a promise of returns. Examiners can read the by-law differently and by-laws change, so the city has the final word.

Please note: Schematic test fit. Not for permit or construction. Zoning and Ontario Building Code read to be confirmed with the municipality. Costs and rents are allowances and market comparables, not a quantity surveyor's estimate or an appraisal.

Questions

Why "beta"?

We're running the first studies now and refining the format with early clients. Pricing is quoted per site until it settles, and your feedback shapes what goes in the package.

What do I need to send?

The address is enough to start. A survey, any existing drawings, your target unit count and bedroom mix, and your budget make the test fit more accurate.

How long does it take?

It depends on the level and the site. The delivery date is set in your quote, before you pay anything.

How do I pay?

Accept the quote on your job page and pay the deposit by card. The balance is due when the package is ready, the same way as our drafting jobs.

Are minor variance applications included?

No. The study can show an option that needs variances and what those would be, but the application itself is separate.
