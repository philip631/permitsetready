How it works

  1. 1Send the siteThe address, the survey if you have it, your target unit count and bedroom mix, your budget, and the status of the site.
  2. 2We set the optionsWe review zoning and the Building Code for the lot and define the options worth testing.
  3. 3You decideYou get the PDF plan set, the feasibility workbook, and a one-page summary listing what to confirm with the city.

What it is

An early, schematic read of what a site can support and how the options compare, to help you scope the next step before investing in full drawings.

What it isn't

It's not a permit set, a zoning approval, an appraisal or investment advice, and it doesn't replace your own due diligence. Examiners can read the by-law differently and by-laws change, so the city has the final word.

Please note: Schematic test fit. Not for permit or construction. Zoning and Ontario Building Code read to be confirmed with the municipality. Costs and rents are allowances and market comparables, not a quantity surveyor's estimate or an appraisal.

Questions

Why "beta"?

We're running the first studies now and refining the format with early clients. Pricing is quoted per site until it settles, and your feedback shapes what goes in the package.

What do I need to send?

The address is enough to start. A survey, any existing drawings, your target unit count and bedroom mix, and your budget make the test fit more accurate.

How long does it take?

It depends on the level and the site. The delivery date is set in your quote, before you pay anything.

How do I pay?

By card on your job page when you accept the quote. Feasibility packages are paid in full up front, plus HST.

Are minor variance applications included?

No. The study can show an option that needs variances and what those would be, but the application itself is separate.
