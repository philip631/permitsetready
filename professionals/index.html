Questions from firms

Will my client ever know?

No. The set goes out in your title block and line styles, under your name. We never contact your client or the municipality.

Who is responsible for the permit?

You are, as the designer or engineer of record. You review, stamp or seal, and submit. If the examiner comments, send them over and we revise; one round is included.

What do you need from me to start?

The design (sketch, markups or SD drawings with dimensions), the survey, any existing drawings for renovations, your deadline, and your template and a recent set so we can match your standards.

How do I send a job?

Book it, then reply to the receipt with a Drive or Dropbox link. Fixed quote within one business day.

Which municipalities do you cover?

Mississauga, Toronto, Oakville, Burlington, Brampton, Halton Hills, Innisfil, Barrie, Guelph, Kitchener, Hamilton and many more. If it's in Ontario and under Part 9 of the Building Code, send it through.
