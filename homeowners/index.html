The question everyone asks

Why isn't your company name on my drawings?

Ontario's Building Code lets a homeowner act as the designer for work on their own house. You sign the permit forms as the owner, and the drawings go in under your name.

We're the drafting help behind you: we draw it to scale, check it against your zoning and the 2024 Building Code, and give you a submission checklist. You skip the design-firm fee, and you stay in control of your own application.

Want a registered designer to sign instead? That's our "Designed for you" option below.

Homeowner questions

Can I really apply for my own permit?

Yes. For work on your own house, Ontario lets the owner be the applicant and the designer. Most municipalities take applications through an online portal; our checklist walks you through yours.

Do you talk to the City for me?

You're the applicant and the City's contact. If the examiner sends comments, upload them to your project page and we revise the drawings for you to resubmit.

How do I follow my project?

Log in to your project page with your email. You'll see where your drawings are, any questions from us, and your download once they're ready.

What if my project needs an engineer?

We flag it in the pre-screen (removed walls, new beams, underpinning, additions) and tell you what the engineering will cost before you commit, so there are no surprises.

What if I don't have drawings of my house?

Our measuring partner visits and measures the house as it is today. The cost depends on the size of the house: enter your home's square footage and answer a few quick questions when you start your project for an instant estimate.
