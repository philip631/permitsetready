Terms for drafting jobs and set reviews

These terms apply to every drafting job you book and every permit set review you buy from PermitSetReady ("we"). Ticking the box when you book a job or buy a review means you, and the firm you act for ("you"), accept them. We record the date and the version you accepted.

1. Drafting: what we do

  • We prepare drafting and permit drawing sets for Ontario residential projects (Part 9) in accordance with the 2024 Ontario Building Code, from the design, survey, markups and instructions you provide.
  • We deliver PDF and DWG files, plus a one-page job notes sheet listing our assumptions, open questions and any Building Code or zoning concerns we flagged.
  • Basement lowering: we draft underpinning and benching drawings from the existing basement layout and our standard details (or from your engineer's design if you send it), for your engineer to review and stamp. The engineer is responsible for the design, sequencing, shoring, field reviews and any effect on neighbouring buildings. One round of the engineer's markups is included once the job is paid in full, if uploaded within 6 weeks of the final files; a change of method or design, and any further rounds, are billed at $85/hour. Coordination with neighbours, including party walls, is the owner's and contractor's responsibility.
  • Design from a description: if you don't have a design yet, we prepare a layout from your written description for you to approve before drafting, quoted per job or at $85/hour.
  • Title block: drafted in your template and title block if you provide one, or in our clean, unbranded format. Either way, our name does not appear on your drawings, files or file properties.
  • If you want your own template, you send it with your first job (ideally with 1–2 recent permit sets, in Revit or DWG, and your text, dimension and line styles). We set it up and send a sample sheet for you to approve before drafting. Template setup is a one-time fee of $300, added to that first job; later jobs in your template have no setup fee.
  • Before final files are released, you get a watermarked draft to check. Changes you ask for on the draft are included. Code revisions the examiner asks for in City comments are included. Design changes after delivery are billed at $85/hour.

2. What stays with you

  • You are responsible for the project and its permit submission. Design decisions, the BCIN or seal, and the final review of the drawings for Building Code and by-law compliance are yours. We draft to the Code and flag what we notice, but we do not sign, stamp or take design responsibility.
  • You are responsible for the accuracy of the information you give us: dimensions, survey, existing conditions and engineering.
  • We check zoning, but approval rests with the municipality. Examiners can interpret a by-law differently and by-laws change, so zoning approval cannot be guaranteed. Minor variances are not included.
  • Engineering, HVAC design and anything outside Part 9 are by others unless your engineer supplies the design.

3. Prices and payment

  • Prices are in Canadian dollars, plus HST, and are shown when you book. Your price or fixed quote is sent within one business day after we pre-screen your files; quoted work can instead be billed at $85/hour, as you choose. A code that covers the full deposit covers the whole job.
  • Deposit to start: 50% of the job price on your first job, 25% on every job after that.
  • The balance is due when you approve the draft. Final files are released once it is paid.
  • Payment is by card through Stripe. Referral credits are applied automatically at checkout.

4. No refunds

All payments are final and non-refundable, including deposits, balances, template setup, single reviews and membership fees. If you cancel a job after you start it, the deposit is kept and work already done is not refunded or credited.

5. Permit set reviews

  • A review is a quality check to help your submission. It is not a permit approval, an engineering review or a design service. The designer of record stays responsible for the drawings.
  • A single review includes one re-check of the same set within 30 days.
  • Memberships renew monthly until cancelled. You can cancel from the billing page at any time; it ends at the close of the period already paid. Unused reviews don't carry over.

6. Referral rewards

When a firm you refer pays for its first review or drafting job, you each choose one reward: a free set review or a $150 credit on a drafting job. Rewards have no cash value, can't be transferred, and are given once per referred firm.

7. Confidentiality and ownership

  • We keep your files, client details and office standards confidential and use them only for your jobs. Your template is never shared with other firms, and your drawings are never used in our marketing.
  • We never contact your client, the municipality or your consultants unless you ask us to.
  • Once a job is paid in full, its PDF and DWG files are yours to use. Our models, families, details and drafting methods stay ours; the Revit template we build from your standards is used only for your jobs.
  • Construction details we create or adapt for your jobs are added to our detail library. You give us permission to reuse them on other projects, with your name, logo, client and project information removed.

8. Timing

Standard delivery is 4–7 business days, and rush is 3 business days, counted from receiving complete information and any payment due. Missing or unclear information stops the clock until you answer.

Jobs waiting on you. When a job is waiting on you (answers to our questions, the deposit, your check of the draft, or the balance), we send reminders. If we don't hear from you for 45 days, we close the job; you'll get a notice 7 days before. You can reopen a closed job from your job page within 6 months of closing and carry on where it left off, with any deposit applied; after that it is booked as a new job. Deposits on closed jobs are not refunded (section 4). A job with an unpaid balance is not closed: the final files stay locked until it is paid.

9. Liability

Our total liability for any job or review is limited to the fee paid for it. We are not liable for permit refusals, delays, construction costs, or claims arising from the design or its review.

Schematic Design feasibility packages (beta). A feasibility package is a planning tool, not a promise. Its plans, zoning and Building Code read, costs, rents and values are early estimates built on the information you give us, public sources and the assumptions listed in the package, and they change with the site, the market and the municipality's own review. "Schematic test fit. Not for permit or construction. Zoning and Ontario Building Code read to be confirmed with the municipality. Costs and rents are allowances and market comparables, not a quantity surveyor's estimate or an appraisal." It is not legal, financial, appraisal or investment advice. Any decision to acquire, sell, finance, design or build a property is yours, made with your own advisers and due diligence, and we are not liable for any loss arising from it or from reliance on the package. Feasibility packages are paid in full when the quote is accepted.

10. Changes and ending

  • We may update these terms. The version you accepted when you booked a job or bought a review applies to that job or review.
  • Either side can stop working together by email at any time. Jobs already started are finished and paid as agreed.
  • These terms are governed by the laws of Ontario.
